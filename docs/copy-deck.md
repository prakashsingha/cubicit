## Automation & AI `/practices/automation`

**H1.** Automate the grind. Keep people on judgment.

**Sub.** Cubic automates work that should not still be retyped. That includes classic RPA for stable, rules-based steps, workflow between systems, and supervised AI when the input is messy (PDFs, email, tickets). The point is time back, not a bot for the board deck.

**From the live cards, updated.**

- **Automate tasks.** High-volume, stable steps: RPA and APIs, not a model guessing a ledger.
- **Streamline processes.** Cut the waits between systems and teams. Orchestrate, don’t swivel-chair.
- **Increase employee productivity.** Specialists spend time on exceptions and customers. AI drafts; people decide.
- **Improve customer experiences.** Faster cycle time without a larger headcount, and without silent failures from an unattended agent.

**H2.** What we will and will not do

- Will: RPA on deterministic processes, integrations, human-in-the-loop extraction and classification.
- Will not: unsupervised agents on payments, PHI, or anything you cannot rewind. `[TBD: Cubic’s hard no-go list]`

**H2.** When to call us

Operations work that is painful, repeated, and still done by people who should be doing judgment work. A process you can describe. A system of record we can hook.
