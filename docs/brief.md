# Cubic Technologies — redesign brief (draft)

Status: draft for design. Facts in plain text are from the live site. Anything in `[PROOF]` or `[TBD]` is not claimed until Cubic confirms it.

## Job of the site

Convince two people in one visit:

1. A hiring leader that Cubic can put **highly experienced specialists** in the room, then stay accountable through interview and start.
2. A senior candidate that Cubic is a serious house, not a volume mill.

Secondary job: make the other three practices feel like real offers, not leftover cards.

## Positioning

Cubic Technologies is a Dallas technology firm that **staffs senior software talent** and **delivers the work those people are hired to do**: product engineering, communications platforms, and automation (including AI where it is actually useful).

Through-line: *We know the work because we do the work. We staff the people who can do it.*

Not: a generic “digital experiences” agency. Not: a job board. Not: an “AI company” with no craft behind it.

## What we are updating from the old site

The live cards are from ~2019. Keep the four practices. Refresh how they are named and scoped:

- **Staffing** still leads. Senior people who can ship in teams that already use AI tools. We do not claim a magic matching engine unless `[PROOF]`.
- **Software** drops empty “emerging technology.” Applied AI in the product (search, copilots, document workflows) is in scope when it has an owner, evals, and a rollback. Agents are not the default architecture.
- **Communications** keeps VoIP. Add intelligent routing, assist, and transcript workflows. A bot is not the product.
- **Automation** absorbs classic RPA and adds modern workflow + supervised AI on messy inputs (email, PDFs, tickets). Unsupervised agents on production data are out unless Cubic later proves a specific offer.
